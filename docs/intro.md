# Welcome to Zelcore

Zelcore is your **secure and simple** multi-chain cryptocurrency wallet. Take complete control of your digital assets with true self-custody across 80+ blockchains and 100,000+ tokens.

## What is Zelcore?

Zelcore is a non-custodial, multi-asset wallet that empowers you to:

- **Store** your crypto assets securely with self-custody
- **Exchange Features** - Trade across 1,200+ currency pairs with built-in swaps
- **Advanced Features** - Manage your portfolio across multiple blockchains
- **Connect** to DeFi applications via WalletConnect
- **Track** your NFTs on Ethereum, Solana, and Polygon
- **Operate** FluxNodes for the Flux ecosystem

## Why Choose Zelcore?

### True Self-Custody
Your private keys are never stored or shared—only you have complete access to your assets.

### Multi-Platform Support
Access your wallet seamlessly across:
- **Desktop**: Windows, macOS, Linux
- **Mobile**: iOS and Android
- **Browser**: Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Brave

### Security Features
- 24-word seed phrase for maximum recovery security
- Decentralized 2FA (d2FA) with distributed authentication
- Biometric login support
- Always-on encryption
- Independently audited by Cure53

### Extensive Blockchain Support
- 80+ blockchains including Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, and more
- 100,000+ tokens supported
- 100M+ NFTs across major networks

## Trusted by 500K+ Users

Founded in 2018 by InFlux Technologies Limited, Zelcore has established itself as a reliable and feature-rich cryptocurrency wallet with weekly updates and continuous improvements.
