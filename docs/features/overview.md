# Features Overview

Zelcore offers a comprehensive suite of features designed to give you complete control over your crypto assets. From basic wallet functions to advanced trading and node management, Zelcore has everything you need.

Background: [Zelcore Pro Features Most Users Miss](https://zelcore.io/academy/how-to/pro-moves-zelcore-features)

## Core Wallet Features

### Multi-Chain Support

Manage assets across 80+ blockchains (as of October 2026) from a single interface:

- **Bitcoin** and Bitcoin-like chains (BCH, LTC, DOGE, etc.)
- **Ethereum** and EVM-compatible chains (Polygon, BSC, Avalanche, etc.)
- **Solana, Tron, Cardano, XRP, Cosmos**, and many more
- Chain guides: [TRON and TRC-20](/docs/guides/tron-wallet-guide), [Polygon](/docs/guides/polygon-wallet-guide), [FLUX](/docs/guides/flux-wallet-guide), [Kadena](/docs/guides/kadena-kda-guide), [Monero (XMR)](/docs/guides/monero-xmr) and [ERC-20 tokens](/docs/guides/managing-erc20-tokens)
- Support for 100,000+ tokens across all chains

### Portfolio Management

Track your entire crypto portfolio in one place:

- Real-time balance updates across all chains
- Portfolio value in multiple fiat currencies
- Historical performance charts
- Asset allocation visualization
- Automatic token discovery
- Send and receive on every supported chain: see [Send and Receive Crypto in Zelcore](/docs/guides/send-and-receive)
- Bring funds from another wallet: see [Move Funds from Another Wallet to Zelcore](/docs/guides/moving-funds-from-other-wallets)

### Multi-Platform Sync

Access your wallet seamlessly across devices:

- [Desktop](https://zelcore.io/wallet) (Windows, macOS, Linux)
- [Mobile](https://zelcore.io/mobile-wallet) (iOS, Android)
- [Browser Extension](https://zelcore.io/browser-extension) (Chrome, Edge, Brave; Firefox is not available yet). See [Zelcore Browser Extension](/docs/guides/browser-extension)

## Trading & Exchange

### Built-in Swap

Exchange cryptocurrencies directly within Zelcore:

- Swap between 800+ cryptocurrencies and tokens (October 2026)
- Offers from several swap providers, including centralized exchange services and DEX and cross-chain routers, so you can compare rates
- Cross-chain swaps
- No account registration required
- Each offer's rate already includes the provider's fees and any partner fee; the network fee is shown separately before you confirm
- Which pairs you can swap, and the minimum and maximum amounts, depend on the providers available when you ask for a quote
- How to swap, fees and min/max limits: see [Swap Crypto in Zelcore: Fees and Limits](/docs/guides/swaps-fees-and-limits)

### Tokenized US Stocks (bStocks)

Hold and trade tokenized US equities next to your crypto:

- Dozens of US stocks and ETFs as BEP-20 tokens on BNB Smart Chain, each backed 1:1 by the real share
- Buy and sell with USDT or BNB through the built-in swap (Binance Web3 provider)
- Live prices, charts and dividend-adjusted balances
- Fully self-custodial, no Binance account required
- Availability depends on your country. See the [bStocks guide](/docs/guides/bstocks-tokenized-stocks-guide)

### Buy Crypto with Fiat

Purchase cryptocurrency using traditional payment methods:

- Credit/debit card and bank transfer, depending on the provider and your country
- Third-party providers such as MoonPay, Guardarian, Topper, Onramper and FinchPay (October 2026)
- The providers, payment methods and limits you see depend on your country and amount
- Each provider runs its own identity checks, and fees are included in the quote shown before you continue
- Buy and Sell are available on desktop, Android and iOS
- Step by step: see [Buy Crypto with Fiat in Zelcore](/docs/guides/buying-crypto)

### Price Tracking

Stay informed with real-time market data:

- Live price updates
- 24h price changes
- Market cap information

## Security Features

### Self-Custody

Your keys, your crypto:

- Private keys never leave your device
- Non-custodial architecture
- No company access to your funds
- Complete financial sovereignty

### Decentralized 2FA (d2FA)

Next-generation authentication:

- A numeric PIN you create in the Flux ID app, stored encrypted on the Flux blockchain rather than on a Zelcore server
- Asked at login, before secure tasks such as sending and swapping, or both
- Zelcore can't reset a lost PIN, so back it up
- Optional but highly recommended
- Setup, PIN prompts and errors: see [Set Up d2FA in Zelcore](/docs/security/d2fa)

### Biometric Authentication

Convenient and secure access:

- Face ID / Touch ID (iOS)
- Fingerprint authentication (Android)
- Windows Hello support (Desktop)
- Local authentication only

### Hardware Wallet Integration

Maximum security for large holdings:

- Ledger support on desktop over USB (no Bluetooth), including Kaspa
- Trezor support on desktop (experimental; needs Trezor Bridge or the Trezor Suite desktop app)
- Sign transactions on hardware device
- View-only mode for cold storage
- Setup and supported coins: see [Ledger and Trezor on Zelcore Desktop](/docs/guides/hardware-wallets)

### Security Audit

Independently verified security:

- Audited by Cure53
- Regular security updates
- Transparent security practices
- Weekly software updates

## Advanced Features

### Tax & CSV Export

Export your transaction history for tax tools or your accountant:

- One export for every coin, opened from the Export CSV button on a coin's screen or from **Tax & CSV export** in the wallet menu
- Pick the wallets, coins and period, then save one combined file or one file per coin
- Fiat values and layouts for Koinly, CoinTracker, CoinLedger, CoinTracking, Blockpit, ZenLedger, TokenTax and more, plus neutral and accountant layouts
- Save as CSV, XLSX or JSON, with custom column mappings and an address list for tax tools that sync by address
- Zelcore classic and Koinly layouts are free. On mobile and the browser extension, other tax-tool layouts, fiat values and custom column mappings need Zelcore+. In desktop 8.39.0 all layouts are free.
- See the [Tax & CSV Export walkthrough](/docs/walkthroughs/tax-csv-export/)

### Kaspa

Use Kaspa and its ecosystem from Zelcore:

- Send KAS or KRC-20 tokens to a KNS name such as `alice.kas`
- Find your KRC-20 tokens, and consolidate UTXOs when an address holds many small ones
- Import a Kaspa address from a Ledger device on Zelcore desktop and send KAS
- View Kaspa covenant tokens (KRON and KCC-20) at your address
- Sign messages with Kaspa addresses
- Bridge KAS to Igra L2 and iKAS back to KAS
- See the [Kaspa walkthrough](/docs/walkthroughs/kaspa-wallet/) and the [Igra Bridge walkthrough](/docs/walkthroughs/igra-bridge/)

### FluxNodes

Operate nodes for the Flux ecosystem:

- Monitor node status
- Manage multiple nodes
- Delete a node to free its collateral at once, without a transaction
- Node map for the Fluxnodes and Firo networks: hover a location for its nodes, click a country to filter, and use **Only my nodes** to see just yours. It follows the night or light theme and opens at once from saved data
- Earnings tracking
- One-click setup tools
- Comprehensive node analytics

### Earn (Staking)

Stake from the **Earn** screen. Walkthroughs are available for these chains:

- [Stake Ethereum](/docs/walkthroughs/ethereum-staking/) through the Chorus One Max Vault
- [Stake Solana (SOL)](/docs/walkthroughs/staking-solana/) with the Chorus One staking provider
- [Stake FLUX](/docs/walkthroughs/staking-flux/) with lockup tiers and Auto-Reinvest

### Fusion Technology

Convert Flux assets across blockchains:

- Move assets between different chains
- Seamless blockchain interoperability
- Support for Flux ecosystem tokens

### Multi-Wallet Support

Create and manage multiple wallet profiles:

- Separate wallets for different purposes
- Easy switching between wallets
- Independent security settings per wallet
- Portfolio isolation

### Address Book

Save frequently used addresses:

- Quick access to common recipients
- Nickname addresses for easy identification
- Reduce errors in transactions
- Import/export address lists
